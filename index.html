Jiaming He

I am a Ph.D. student at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), advised by Prof. Xudong Jiang. My research lies in Trustworthy AI, with a particular emphasis on holistic red-teaming for advanced generative AI and safety evaluation of agentic AI systems operating in both virtual and physical environments.

I have worked on adversarial attacks, backdoor attacks, data poisoning, unlearnable examples, privacy protection, and the security of large language and multimodal foundation models.

Research

My long-term goal is to build robust and reliable AI systems that remain safe under adversarial pressure. I am currently interested in holistic safety evaluation for agentic AI, including long-horizon failures, decision-chain robustness, and privacy risks in real and simulated environments.
