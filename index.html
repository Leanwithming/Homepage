01 · Research

Stress-testing intelligence,
from pixels to agents.

I study how learning systems fail under adversarial pressure, how those failures propagate through real workflows, and how we can evaluate and mitigate them.

R / 01

Agentic AI Safety

Safety evaluation for agents that perceive, reason, use tools, and act across virtual and physical environments.

  • Long-horizon failure analysis
  • Decision-chain robustness
  • Embodied and computer-use agents
R / 02

Holistic Red-Teaming

Automated and temporal red-teaming for advanced generative models, beyond isolated prompts and outputs.

  • Text-to-video models
  • LLMs and multimodal systems
  • Backdoor and adversarial attacks
R / 03

Privacy by Design

Protecting people and data from unauthorized learning, extraction, personalization, and inversion.

  • Unlearnable examples
  • Agent privacy
  • Video and RAG privacy
